I want you to create a promotional film poster that depicts a alien monument towering over a group of scientist in a dusty desert environment. Make the monument a large stone alien face with high contrast using shadows across the face . The face should many organic lines to contrast the rest of the composition. The shadows should also cast over most of the environment with the alien face having the largest block of positive space  giving the monument a foreboding mood. Have the scientist drag some  hoses making lines drawing the viewers attention to them. Have the scientist grouped together and have hoses make straight lines that make a triangular shape. Make the environment have a jagged sharp shapes that is reminiscent of thorns  giving the monument a hostile undertone.  

Vocabulary Breakdown
Lines are the connection between two points. Lines can be real or implied and they can be geometric or organic.
Shapes are a two dimensional area defined by an enclosed line. Like lines they can be real or implied and geometric or organic. 
Texture is how we perceive something would feel if we could touch it even if its texture is just implied.
Volume is the space an object occupies.
Contrast is when two of the same art elements are used in very different ways to show the difference between them.
