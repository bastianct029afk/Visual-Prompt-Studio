Assignment Title: Visual Prompt Studio 1.2

Image Prompt 

I am a lead graphic designer hired by a major music label and need to create a retro 1980s album cover. I want it to be reminiscent of tron movie poster with a sunset flanked by mountain ranges on both side and two characters in the center of the composition. Have the two characters cast shadows directed away from the horizon to give the composition a somber mood. Put two light bike riders coming down from both upper corners to the center of the composition to create balance with light beams that decay behind them to imply motion. Make the composition mostly warm colors to have unity across the composition. Make the bike riders cool colors to add variety and contrast the warm colors of the composition. Put a neon grid pattern at the bottom of the composition stretching all the way to the sunset to give a sense of scale and depth. 

Vocab

Value
Space
Unity
Balance
Rhythm
